Millvane Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17

Millvane (“the app”) is a desktop application for CNC CAM, G-code simulation and program comparison, published by Opus Ferri.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry.

Network

The Microsoft Store version of the app does not connect to the internet on its own. Updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft’s privacy statement.

Reports you choose to send

If you use Help → Submit a report, only the text and screenshots you add are sent, and only when you press Send.

Your files

CAD models, G-code programs, setups, the library and the settings you create stay on your computer. The app reads them only to do the work you ask for. The library and projects in your Documents folder are kept when you uninstall, unless you choose to remove them.

Children

The app is a technical tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends any information, this policy will be updated before that version is published.
